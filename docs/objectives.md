## Objectives

The primary objectives of **DataLang** are to:

1. **Design a Domain-Specific Language (DSL)** for performing common data-analysis operations using a concise and intuitive syntax.

2. **Develop a lexical analyzer** to identify and classify DataLang tokens such as keywords, identifiers, literals, operators, and delimiters.

3. **Develop a parser** to validate the syntax of DataLang programs according to the defined grammar rules.

4. **Design semantic rules** for data-analysis operations to ensure that DataLang programs are logically and semantically valid.

5. **Maintain a symbol table** to store and manage information about identifiers, datasets, columns, and their associated data types.

6. **Generate an Intermediate Representation (IR)** for DataLang statements to provide an intermediate form suitable for program execution.

7. **Execute valid DataLang programs** to perform data-analysis and visualization operations on the specified datasets.

8. **Provide meaningful error messages** for invalid DataLang programs, including lexical, syntax, semantic, and runtime errors.
